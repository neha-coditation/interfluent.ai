02 · WHAT SHIPPING WELL NEEDS

One picture of the system that all three rooms can read.

Start with what the business actually cares about: product capabilities. Put them next to the services that implement them, instead of a slide that gets redrawn every quarter.

Then add intent. Requirements, briefs and documentation become nodes in the same model as classes, methods and tests. A business question and a technical question now share an answer path.

Once everything is connected, a change can be priced before a date is promised, and the record can be rewritten from the model when the system moves. The next change gets cheaper than the last.
