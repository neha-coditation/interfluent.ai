COMMON QUESTIONS
What counts as a system?

One product and the repositories, documents and requirements that describe it. Most teams have one. A platform company may have several.

Does our code leave our environment?

Team runs in our cloud with encryption at rest and no training on your data. Enterprise can run in your VPC.

Which IDE do we need?

Any. Interfluent decides and governs the change; your IDE and pipelines write and ship it as they do today.
