ABOUT US

We got tired of redrawing the map by hand.

Every product organisation we've worked in began planning the same way: someone senior spent weeks reading the codebase so they could tell the business what it actually did. The map was stale before the first sprint ended. We built the tool we wished we'd had.

WHAT WE BELIEVE
Intent is part of the system.

Requirements, briefs and documentation belong in the same model as classes and tests. If they live elsewhere, they drift.

Price before you promise.

A change should come back as a blast radius with evidence, not a t-shirt size argued over in a meeting.

Humans accept, agents propose.

Nothing an agent writes goes live unreviewed. Every change is a diff someone can reject.

THE TEAM

Built by people who have sat in all three rooms.

Product leads, analysts and engineers who have each been the one asked "how big is this change?" without a good answer. We're a small team and we ship every week.
