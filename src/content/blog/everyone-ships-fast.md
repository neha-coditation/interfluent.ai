Walk into any product company this year and each room looks faster. Business drafts strategy with an assistant. Product turns a conversation into a PRD in an afternoon. Engineering merges forty changes a day.

## The bottleneck moved

When producing an artefact was slow, the handoff was a small part of the cycle. Now the artefact is nearly free and the handoff is most of the cycle.

## What a fix has to do

It has to hold capabilities, requirements, documentation and code in one model, so a business question and a technical question share an answer path.
